Onyx samples

Upstream's own samples from enyojs/onyx at tag 2.5.1-release, ported to ES modules — plus Section List, which is this fork's own kind rather than a port. Each one opens on its own page; use your browser's Back button to come back here.

Not loading? These pages resolve bare specifiers through an import map, so the enyo, layout, onyx and enyo-ilib checkouts have to sit side by side under the served root. Serve their common parent with layout/test-layout/serve.py, not python3 -m http.server.
